# Three portfolio studies

Start the local review server from the repository root with `node concepts/preview.mjs`. The experiments live on `experiment/three-portfolio-directions`, branched from `10c3174`. They share the licensed fonts and a small entry animation helper. Their compositions, content interactions and motion are separate.

| Direction                                                       | First impression                                                                                   | What to try                                                                                                                                | Main tradeoff                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Afterimage](http://localhost:4186/concepts/cinema/)            | A plum screening room, copper line exposures and a cream film title. The opening spends more space on authorship and mood than engineering evidence.                     |
| [Index Room](http://localhost:4186/concepts/index-room/)        | A vermilion book spine and an asymmetric two-line title. A numbered folio slides out of view.      | Turn between the SeeDL, Infera and PulseShift plates. On a phone the spine becomes a top edge and the index becomes a horizontal selector. | Fastest project discovery. The visitor participates principally by choosing what to read.                                 |
| [Computational study](http://localhost:4186/concepts/bitbench/) | A cobalt instrument with a citron matrix. An 8 × 8 title card contracts into the workbench.        | Edit a pixel, change specimens, and compare the two hidden-layer responses. Each prediction comes from the checkpoint's trained model.     | Strongest demonstration of engineering. It asks the visitor to operate something before reading a conventional biography. |

## Research notes

Arnaud Rocca's project-led landing experience informed the idea of giving each signal study its own screening. Afterimage uses hand-authored wave paths and temporal scrubbing. [Independent portfolio](https://arnaudrocca.fr/) · [Awwwards portfolio entry](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/arnaud-rocca-portfolio)

Stefan Vitasović's discussion of the relationship between typography and interaction suggested treating type as an active part of the composition. Index Room translates that principle into print mechanics, with a fixed spine and turning plates. [Codrops case study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/03/05/case-study-stefan-vitasovic-portfolio-2025/)

Yichen Xie's account of an immediately playable opening informed the computational study's invitation to change one pixel. Its visual response uses real input intensities and computed activations. SeeDL is identified as a related inspection tool. [Codrops case study](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/10/06/yichen-xie-portfolio/)

## Review evidence

The `review/` folder contains a 1440px desktop image, a 390px mobile image and a desktop WebM walkthrough for each concept. Edge recordings include the first-entry transition and a project interaction. The captures were reviewed for silhouette, type hierarchy and mobile fit. The Swiss phone headline and plate stamp were adjusted after the first inspection. These are concept prototypes, not production accessibility or performance audits.
