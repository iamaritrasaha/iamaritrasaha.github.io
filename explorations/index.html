01 / EARTH SYSTEMS / INTERACTIVE APPLICATION

VECTOR

A planet is a coordinate system.

An independent Earth instrument combining geography, orbital propagation and aircraft observations. VECTOR makes the origin and age of the data part of the picture.

Public orbital elements are propagated with SGP4 through Satellite.js. Aircraft positions are observations with variable coverage. Propagated positions are not direct spacecraft telemetry, and interpolation is not a new observation.

A circular orbit, in its own plane

x = r cos θ, y = r sin θ

A coordinate sketch only. The application uses SGP4, not this simplified circular path.

03 / PUBLIC FINANCE / DATA VISUALIZATION

Arthrekha

A number needs a source.

A public-finance explorer connecting India’s Union Budget to official source documents and inspectable derivations, with English and Bengali explanations.

Annual Budget Estimates and provisional cumulative actuals answer different questions. Arthrekha keeps their scope, reporting periods and provenance visible instead of presenting them as interchangeable numbers.

A proportion with a defined scope

share = part / total × 100%

Use the same period, units and accounting scope in both numerator and denominator.

Vyren

Follow the connection.

I want to know which application opened a connection, where it went and how certain that attribution is. Vyren connects kernel and packet evidence with live telemetry and local history.

I separated the Rust telemetry engine, Flutter interface and protected inspection helpers. Unknown evidence stays unknown.

Accounting for what was observed

Bobserved = Battributed + Bunknown

Bytes assigned to an application, plus bytes we cannot assign.
Unknown stays in the equation.
